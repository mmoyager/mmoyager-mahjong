# MJ and 麻雀格闘倶楽部: UI/UX field notes for a browser riichi client

**Method:** every claim is tied to a page I opened with `web_fetch`. Where my only evidence is a screenshot caption, a Shift-JIS-garbled page, forum recollection, or a search snippet, I say so and mark it **approximate**. I could not verify any title named 「麻雀格闘倶楽部 スリーサウザンド」 in Konami's own series history ([MFC 20th history](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/20th/history/)); the nearest entries are 「麻雀格闘倶楽部 DS Wi-Fi対応」 and 「麻雀格闘倶楽部 touch」. I therefore do not describe it.

## A. セガNET麻雀 MJ / MJ ARCADE

### A1. Layout
A **single full-screen 3D table** with floating UI, no app-like chrome. Tap-tabs (案内 / 個人情報 / 試合状況) overlay information; the table stays primary. The **情報エリア** sits on the right showing GP and credits, with treasure/evolution drops on the left, and a **chat tab on the far-left edge** ([便利機能](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/function/index.html)). The **table centre** holds the wall counter and all four scores — press and hold to swap scores for **point differences** plus a provisional SCORE. Action buttons appear **bottom-right** as a contextual window ([MJ4 基本操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control.html)); the 全鳴有/ドラ鳴 toggle is also bottom-right, while detailed-call buttons sit **bottom-left** on wide cabinets ([操作方法](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/op/index.html)).

MJ4 documents the older geometry: **情報エリア（上）** = shop event / mode / rules; **情報エリア（下）** = dan, event-league and shop-event results ([MJ4 基本操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control.html)). Net drift is stacked top/bottom panels → left/right frame with tap-tabs. Sega's own framing is spectacle-first: "ド派手な演出" plus an "アツイ実況" commentary layer ([MJ ARCADEとは？](https://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/about/)) — arcade spectacle with information bolted on, not a dense log table. Own hand: 3D tiles with **rendered hands** ("麻雀牌や雀卓、プレイヤーの手は3Dで描写される") ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%82%BB%E3%82%ACNET%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80+MJ)); whether it is centred or left-aligned, or ever two rows, is **approximate** — no text source.

### A2. Information design
Always on: remaining wall + all four scores in the centre; per-seat plate with dan, nickname and avatar, tappable for that player's profile. Dora indicators are at the **top-left** ("画面左上のドラ表示牌") ([MJ4 基本操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control.html)). Round wind / hand number / honba rendering is **approximate** — screenshot-only.

MJ's info layer is *interrogative*, not printed. The 試合状況 tab holds 対局の推移 (point-trend graph, win and deal-in counts), a **1位獲得条件** panel computing the han/fu needed to take first, and a 乱数情報 panel exposing the "プレイヤー乱数シード" and "不確定要素" ([便利機能](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/function/index.html)). Wall generation is documented publicly, with an online **牌山Viewer** ([MJ Viewer / 牌山](http://mj.sega.jp/mj5evo/viewer/index.html)).

**Replay is first-class.** After a match a QR code appears; scanning opens the **MJ Viewer** replay in a browser or the MJ app, with URL and embed code for sharing ([MJ Viewer](http://mj.sega.jp/mj5evo/viewer/index.html)). In-store, the **ライブモニター** plays curated replays with original 実況・解説 voice, and a replay QR appears via 決定ボタン＋左ボタン ([ライブモニター](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/live/index.html)). A Ver.8.4 update reportedly added アーカイブ観戦 and app-side replay — **approximate, snippet-only** ([APPLION](https://applion.jp/event/93329/) returned 403).

### A3. Tile and table rendering
Fully 3D — tiles, table and hands ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%82%BB%E3%82%ACNET%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80+MJ)) — and MJ4 Evolution shipped a **transparent ガラス牌 rule**, i.e. real material work rather than sprites ([MJ4 Evolution](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/sp_evo.html)). The surface is **cosmetic, not fixed**: item groups are 「フェイス・ボディ」「ハンド」「枠」「背景」「表面」, and table backgrounds are equippable per event with a 一括/モード別 selector ([表示設定](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/custom/display.html); [4Gamer Ver9.4](https://www.4gamer.net/games/226/G022696/20250325037/)). A default green felt is folklore — **approximate**.

Two implementable details: **捨て牌整列表示** — turning it ON aligns discards neatly and stops them being "逆向きに表示", meaning the default deliberately rotates each seat's pond and this toggle flattens it ([表示設定](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/custom/display.html)). Melds and discards are marked by **brief tap → blue mark**, same tap to remove ([便利機能](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/function/index.html)). The sideways riichi tile is **approximate** — undescribed.

### A4. Input model
**Arcade:** touch-first, but the cabinet also has **physical buttons** covering discard, call and win: "タッチパネルを使用しなくても、手元のボタンを使うことで、捨て、鳴き、アガリなどの、基本操作を行うことができます" ([MJ4 基本操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control.html)). It is not a flat ツモ/ロン/リーチ/ポン/チー/カン row: the call buttons switch between 「通常」 and 「特殊」. In 通常 they perform calls; in 特殊 you hold a button and tap a hand tile to attach 鳴有/二鳴/鳴無 permission, and a dedicated **アガリボタン** opens the wait window for アガリ牌指定 and re-displays waits after timeout ([MJ4 上級者向け操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control_expert.html); [MJ4 サポート機能](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control_support.html)).

**Touch/PC:** discard is two-tap confirm ("牌は2回タッチで捨てます"); a hard tap is **強打**, with 5-step sensitivity and an off switch ([操作方法](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/op/index.html); [操作設定](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/custom/op.html)). Drag only sorts tiles (auto-sort can be OFF), moving up to 4 at once using cabinet buttons ([MJ4 上級者向け操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control_expert.html)). No keyboard map is documented.

**Assists** are spatial, not textual: hold a tile → every visible copy turns **red**; hold two ターツ tiles → accepting tiles turn **yellow**; hold a dora indicator → visible dora **yellow**; hold a riichi stick → every tile passed after that riichi **red** ([便利機能](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/function/index.html)). MJ4 had テンパイ表示 (blue frame on any tenpai-preserving discard), 待ち牌表示 and 満貫チャンス/満貫確定 prompts; it also split auto-play into オートアガリ and オートツモ切り, both default OFF and auto-disabling with an "×" when tenpai breaks ([MJ4 サポート機能](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control_support.html)). **自動操作モード** toggles at tenpai: auto-win, auto-discard anything untsumoable (auto-kan when legal), and **refuses all calls** ([便利機能](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/function/index.html)). Call policy has three states: 全鳴有 / 全鳴無 / **ドラ鳴** ([操作方法](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/op/index.html)).

### A5. Motion and sound
The setting is **演出設定**: one **「演出モード」** switch (シンプル / 標準) plus per-effect overrides. シンプル disables 面子演出, 危険牌演出, 配牌示唆演出, ドラ牌示唆演出, 鳴き/リーチ/打牌/アガリ宣言カットイン, ホワイトアウト演出, シャンテン数良化演出 and the アガリボタン SE ([演出設定](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/custom/effect.html)). This is a genuine reduced-motion mode, with a carve-out: even with cut-ins OFF, ドラポン/カン and 役満 cut-ins still play. Each effect has a named visual: meld/taatsu frames, a danger reticle on a draw while riichi, lightning on a good deal or a shanten-improving draw, a gilded dora-flip frame. Audio is central: a 実況 commentary system and a "ゴッドハンド演出", with English commentary from Ver.5.0 ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%82%BB%E3%82%ACNET%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80+MJ)); Ver.4.4 added 小林未沙 実況 and 馬場裕一 解説 voices ([NetGameBM](https://netgamebm.com/game_news/view/82226), snippet-only). MJ4 Evolution renewed the inter-round and result presentations ([MJ4 Evolution](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/sp_evo.html)), and a 2010 player report notes **tiles physically moving during point exchange** ([Cou氏の徒然日記](https://coublood.hatenablog.com/entry/59331253)).

### A6. Settlement and results
The centre plate doubles as the settlement surface — 点棒 movement, then point differences and provisional SCORE on hold ([MJ4 基本操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control.html)). MJ4 added an unusual evaluation cue: at hand end, opponents' winning tiles that score no evaluation points get a **grey frame** ("評価対象外の他家アタリ牌演出"), and the same-tile highlight expands to the wait window and opponents' hands ([MJ4 サポート機能](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control_support.html)). Long-term: the cabinet shows **打ち筋** (win rate, average value, speed, deal-in) as **5-step grades** plus 戦績 for 本日 / 今月・先月; deeper analysis (win tendency, per-yaku win counts, national comparison) lives in MJ.NET ([戦績・打ち筋](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/system/record/index.html)). Uma is rule-level: 4-player +20/+10/−10/−20; 3-player 35000 start / 40000 return, +20/±0/−20 ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%82%BB%E3%82%ACNET%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80+MJ)).

### A7. MJ vs 雀魂
**Better.** MJ's *queryable table* beats 雀魂's highlighting: hold a tile and every visible copy, taatsu acceptance, visible dora, or everything after a riichi stick lights up in a distinct colour ([便利機能](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/function/index.html)). The 1位獲得条件 panel is a feature 雀魂 lacks entirely. Its **provable-fairness layer** — published 牌山生成 docs, a public 牌山Viewer, and an in-match seed/不確定要素 tab — is a different class of transparency. Call policy is far more expressive: 全鳴有 / 全鳴無 / ドラ鳴 plus per-tile 鳴有・二鳴・鳴無 via 特殊鳴き設定 ([MJ4 上級者向け操作](https://www.sega-mj.com/mj4/control_expert.html)). Replay is a better *social object*: MJ Viewer yields a shareable URL and embed code ([MJ Viewer](http://mj.sega.jp/mj5evo/viewer/index.html)). And 演出モード シンプル with per-effect granularity is a better accessibility story than one animations toggle ([演出設定](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/custom/effect.html)).

**Worse.** The information architecture is hostile to reading: 対局の推移 and 1位獲得条件 hide behind taps, points hide behind press-and-hold, and **no source I opened describes any persistent textual action log** — MJ narrates via 実況 voice instead ([MJ ARCADEとは？](https://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/about/)). Input is slower per turn: two taps to discard, no keyboard map, and a 強打 gesture needing sensitivity tuning ([操作設定](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/custom/op.html)). Auto-play is blunt — it refuses *all* calls, so you cannot auto-discard while still ponning ([便利機能](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/play/function/index.html)). And on-cabinet stats are four letter grades; real analysis is gated behind MJ.NET membership ([戦績・打ち筋](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/system/record/index.html)) — worse than 雀魂's built-in 牌譜/データ screens.

## B. 麻雀格闘倶楽部 (Konami MFC / Extreme / UNION / SP)

### B1. Layout
A **panel-heavy table on a 32-inch touch monitor** (upgraded from 21.5"), with tiles sized to match real automatic-table tiles ("牌の大きさも全自動雀卓の牌の大きさ") ([Konami MFC Extreme](https://www.konami.com/arcadegames/products/am_mfc_extreme/); [4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/334/G033490/20170913064/)). It is **touch-only** — "モード選択からゲームプレーまで全ての操作は画面を直接タッチするだけ" — with no button panel at all, the inverse of MJ. In the SP mobile build **utility buttons run down the right edge**, settings hide behind a top-right gear, and holding the right-side button swaps the readout ([対局の便利機能](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/taikyoku_kinou/index.html)); each player's name carries a **3-step connection indicator** beneath it. Arcade hand/pond/centre geometry is **approximate** — Konami documents behaviour, not coordinates. A 2015 reviewer found the mobile hand area "ぎゅうぎゅう詰め" with hard-to-identify call targets ([麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)), evidence of a dense rather than airy layout. **UNION** (live 2025-10-15) is styled "黒を基調としたラグジュアリーなデザイン", framed as a social club, with the mode-select screen rebuilt for discoverability ([gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/414291); [Konami](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/ac/news/202510/10/news.html)).

### B2. Information design
MFC's signature is **toggleable, state-changing readouts** rather than a fixed HUD. Holding the right-side button cycles the table between **あなたとの点差 / 一位との点差 / シャンテン数 / 起家**; tapping your own score cycles 点差 views one step at a time ([対局の便利機能](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/taikyoku_kinou/index.html)). Draws you discarded are rendered **slightly darker** (ツモ切り牌), encoding tedashi/tsumogiri at a glance. UNION added three named numeric assists: **待ち牌残り数表示**, **同牌強調** (how many of a tile you hold and how many are in the pond), and **向聴数カウント** ([gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/414291)). SP prints the wait's remaining count at the tile's bottom-right; the 2015 review shows waits were displayed then **without** counts ([対局の便利機能](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/taikyoku_kinou/index.html); [麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)). Two removals matter: the **長考ボタン was removed from the match screen in UNION** in favour of automatic 長考 after a fixed interval ([PC操作方法](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/eacloud/p/info/pc_operation.html)), and the beginner countdown was changed from あがり to **テンパイ** in Ver.2.3.0 ([Konami 2025/11/18](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/ac/news/202511/18/news.html)). Replay is strong: SP offers last-50 牌譜 browsing, per-hand replay, long-press fast-forward, 牌譜ID sharing, **name anonymisation** before sharing, mid-hand snapshots that resume replay, and turning any tenpai moment into a public **何切る** quiz others vote on ([牌譜再生](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/haifu/index.html)). Arcade-side, 疾風 moved 各種結果表示 into the プレミアムエリア so they can be read any time ([MFC 20th history](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/20th/history/)); a shareable public replay URL for the arcade build is **approximate** — unverified.

### B3. Tile and table rendering
Konami stresses spectacle: the bigger panel makes the **thunder effect** grander, and cabinet **LED lighting changes colour with match state** ([Konami](https://www.konami.com/arcadegames/products/am_mfc_extreme/); [4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/334/G033490/20170913064/)). Tiles are real-table-sized, implying a fairly flat, near-top-down presentation; whether these are true 3D meshes with perspective or pre-rendered 3D-look sprites is **approximate** — no page states the technique. MJ's explicit "3D" claim ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%82%BB%E3%82%ACNET%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80+MJ)) has no MFC counterpart in my sources. The surface is **cosmetic**: ビジュアルアレンジ lets you customise **プレーヤーパネル, 卓背景 and 牌** via e-amusement, applied next play ([ビジュアルアレンジ](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/playguide/contents/paseli04.html)) — so "MFC felt colour" has no single answer (**approximate**). Rather than dimming melds, MFC *highlights*: **同牌強調** reports copies held and ponded, and SP's 同牌検索 lights every matching visible tile **green** while you hold a tile, including melds and discards ([gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/414291); [対局の便利機能](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/taikyoku_kinou/index.html)). The sideways riichi tile is **approximate** — undescribed.

### B4. Input model
Arcade is touch-only; discard is **select-then-confirm** ("1回目のタッチで牌を選択し、2回目のタッチで実行"), and declining a call means tapping **パス** ([SP 操作方法](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/operate/index.html)). The **PC (コナステ) build has a real keyboard map** — the most directly copyable thing here: click to select, click again to commit; **A** riichi, **S** chi, **D** pon, **F** ankan/minkan, **Space** tsumo/ron, **Z** pass, **X** toggle never-call. Right-click also works (hold to show, move while held to select, release to confirm), and **press-and-hold for 1 second** on the kan or tsumo/ron button enables auto-kan or auto-win ([PC操作方法](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/eacloud/p/info/pc_operation.html)). Assists are **tiered**: the base tier gives アガリAUTO, 鳴無 and (pre-UNION) 長考; the SP **対局アシスト** package adds 狙える役表示 (tap a yaku for detail), 役完成表示, 安全牌表示 (icon on tiles safe vs a riichi player), テンパイ数表示, あなたの番です表示, ツモ切り表示, ドラ表示, メンツ完成表示, 捨てるとテンパイ表示, テンパイ牌表示 and 立直・ツモ等ができなくなる表示 — and it is **disabled in プラチナ/ダイヤモンド and some event tables** ([対局の便利機能](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/taikyoku_kinou/index.html)). A separate テンパイサポート arrows each discard by shanten effect. There is also **代走**: the CPU plays with the screen visible, using only your hand, all discards/melds and dora (explicitly not your score), strength tied to dan rank, with limits. Timing is split into **打牌時間 (white)** and **長考時間 (orange)**; when the discard clock expires the long-think pool drains automatically and refills at hand start, seconds configurable per table ([SP 操作方法](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/operate/index.html)). The 2015 SP build had **no timer gauge**, only a beep 3 seconds out ([麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)).

### B5. Motion and sound
Konami's language is "大胆なグラフィック演出と迫力のサウンド効果" ([MFC playguide](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/playguide/index.html)); the flagship example is a **lightning strike on mangan-or-better**, which the 2015 reviewer calls satisfying and notes comes with **vibration** ([麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)), and the high-grade cabinet amplifies exactly that effect plus state-driven LED ([4Gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/334/G033490/20170913064/)). UNION's notes reference a **登場演出設定画面**, reachable from the mode-select screen ([Konami](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/ac/news/202510/10/news.html)). **Caveat: I could not verify a setting named 演出スピード in MFC** — no page I opened names one. What is documented is 登場演出設定 plus support-feature ON/OFF under プレミアムエリア → 表示設定. Treat "MFC 演出スピード" as **approximate/unverified**.

### B6. Settlement and results
MFC shows a 点棒 exchange with lightning spectacle and vibration at mangan+ ([麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)). UNION **abolished 打ち切り制**, and leftover 供託 now goes to the leader instead of vanishing — previously "「Extreme」までは消滅していた" ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80%E6%A0%BC%E9%97%98%E5%80%B6%E6%A5%BD%E9%83%A8); [Konami 2025/10/30](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/ac/news/202510/30/news3.html)); UNION also adopted **切り上げ満貫**, absent in Extreme ([gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/414291)). The hand-end yaku/han/fu panel layout is **approximate** — undescribed in any source I opened. Long-term: dan rank, full 戦績, 打ち筋 and あがり率 are saved and checkable **after each match**, with more on e-amusement ([MFC playguide](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/playguide/index.html)); the mobile client reports 平均順位, あがり率, 平均アガり翻数, per-mode records and a **last-30-match placement trend chart** ([麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)). Progression is the identity: ファイトオーブ move 4th→1st, so ranking up requires winning, not just avoiding last ([基本ゲームモード](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/playguide/contents/mode_beginner.html)). Uma is point-based: 4-player +5000/−5000 (東風 20000 start, 半荘 25000), 三麻 ±3000 ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80%E6%A0%BC%E9%97%98%E5%80%B6%E6%A5%BD%E9%83%A8)).

### B7. MFC vs 雀魂
**Better.** The assist tiering is the best onboarding model I found in a competitive client: a named 対局アシスト package — 狙える役表示 with tap-for-detail, 安全牌表示 against a riichi, 捨てるとテンパイ表示, 立直・ツモ等ができなくなる表示 — **switched off in the top tables** ([対局の便利機能](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/taikyoku_kinou/index.html)). That is a cleaner skill-segregation answer than 雀魂's uniform client. The orb economy makes first place the only route upward and renders that as a visible transaction ([麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)). The **牌譜→何切る pipeline** — a saved hand becomes a public quiz other players vote on, replays anonymisable before sharing — has no 雀魂 equivalent ([牌譜再生](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/mfc_sp/howto/haifu/index.html)). And the **PC keyboard map (A/S/D/F/Space/Z/X)** with right-click select-confirm is a concrete one-hand scheme ([PC操作方法](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/eacloud/p/info/pc_operation.html)).

**Worse.** The visual language is ageing and cluttered: the 2015 review calls it "やや古臭い" with a hard-to-read table, poor call-target legibility, no timer gauge and unpleasant discard sounds ([麻雀豆腐](https://majandofu.com/online-mahjong-fight-club)) — **approximate** for UNION, but Konami's own "親しみやすい" redesign rationale concedes a discoverability problem ([gamebiz](https://gamebiz.jp/news/414291)). Real analysis sits on the web/e-amusement side rather than in the client ([MFC playguide](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/playguide/index.html)), and support features are toggled inside a "プレミアムエリア" ([Konami](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/ac/news/202510/10/news.html)) — a paid-tier-shaped surface 雀魂 exposes free. Its reduced-motion story is weaker than MJ's: I found no master equivalent to 演出モード シンプル ([MJ 演出設定](http://www.sega-mj.com/arcade/howto/custom/effect.html)). And its rule surface moves underfoot — UNION changed 切り上げ満貫, 赤ドラ counts, 食い替え, 供託, 打ち切り and the 長考 button at once ([Wikipedia ja via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E9%BA%BB%E9%9B%80%E6%A0%BC%E9%97%98%E5%80%B6%E6%A5%BD%E9%83%A8); [Konami](https://p.eagate.573.jp/game/mfc/ac/news/202510/10/news.html)), so a client must not hard-code assumptions.
